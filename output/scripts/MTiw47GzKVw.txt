LSD Dream Emulator is a game that needs no introduction.
LSD is an exploration game that takes players on a surreal journey through dreamlike environments.
Developed by Asmic Ace Entertainment and released for the PlayStation in 1998, the game stands
as a masterpiece of contemporary art, conceived by Japanese artist Osamu Sato.
Rejecting the traditional conventions of gaming, Sato used the PlayStation as a medium to create
an interactive dream diary based on a dream journal of one of his employees spanning
over a decade.
LSD Dream Emulator is a literal place from your dreams.
In the game, players find themselves within a 3D world where they can freely explore
without any specific objectives.
Movement is limited to basic actions such as walking, turning, and running.
When a game is divided into dreams, each one lasting a few minutes long.
When players come in contact with walls or objects, they are randomly placed in various
surreal locations.
